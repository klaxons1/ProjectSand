Tips and tricks:

  1. if the sandbox runs slowly, just zoom in, it will run smoothly (4 times less to process).
  2. click on a tool twice to create a constant flow of it. or use the 'tap' tool, after you have drawn a tap, the first thing that touches it will start flowing.
  3. if you never want to pay for the premium features, click "never" on the "support the developer" page after selecting a locked material :).
  4. notice that you can rotate the sandbox and the toolbox scrolls horizontally to expose the 40+ tools and materials.

Tools:

  • New - clear the sandbox.
  • Rotation - enable or disable rotation sensor.
  • Zoom - set zoom to a few different zoom settings, zoom in to increase the speed of the game a lot.
  • Spouts - enable or disable the 4 spouts (sand, water, crude oil and salt) from the top of the screen.
  • Borders - set whether the edges of the screen are solid or not.
  • Pause - pause and resume the emulation.
  • View - sets the type of vision, currently normal / infrared.

Material properties:

  • Eraser - it erases everything expect indestructible walls that are a part of a puzzle level.
  • Tap - makes a constant flow of the first movable material that touches it. For example, gases, fire, liquids and powders.
  • Wall / stone / metal / glass - solid and resists explosions and/or acid (depending on which it is).
  • Coal / wood / sawdust - burns in contact with fire and turns into ash.
  • Ice - melts into water, and melts faster in contact with salt or saline.
  • Uranium - if it is not kept cool with water, it gets hot and melts, then if not cooled it goes critical, destroying almost everything in the blast, turning it to ash!
  • Wick - absorbs liquids and burns.
  • C4 - solid explosive that makes a huge explosion destroying most things.
  • Wax - melts in contact with fire, and combusts if near something extremely hot.
  • Salt - dissolves in water, melts ice.
  • Plant - grows and is flammable.
  • Firework - explodes sending out rockets.
  • Blackhole - sucks in everything.
  • Filter - allows liquids to go through, but nothing else.
  • Crude oil - flammable liquid.
  • Acid - burns through most things, not glass. also can be boiled into acid vapor that is dangerous!
  • Mercury - heavy liquid metal.
  • Cement powder - dissolves in water, then sets solid.
  • Gunpowder - explodes.
  • Pump - pumps any liquid to the top.
  • Water - freezes, boils and condenses. ice melts in contact with salt or saline.
  • Nitroglycerin - liquid explosive.
  • Methane gas - floats up and explodes into flames if ignited.
  • Algae - grows and spreads through water.
  • Lava - extremely hot liquid stone.
  • petrol - extremely flammable.
  • Paraffin - only flammable if absorbed by something or when touching something extremely hot.
  • Teleportation portal - teleports anything from portal A to portal B. when you draw, the first tap sets the location of portal A, then the second draws both portal A and portal B.
  • Conways game of life - a simple mathematical pattern that spreads like microbial life.
